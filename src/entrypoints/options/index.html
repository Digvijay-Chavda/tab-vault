Smart tab grouping

Use Group tabs in the TabVault popup to group a window's tabs. Tabs are grouped by your rules first, then by category (Dev, Video, Mail, Social…), then by site. Pinned tabs and tabs already in a group are never touched. Everything runs on your device.
